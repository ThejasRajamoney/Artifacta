Privacy

No Data Collection

Artifacta does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no user identification.

No Telemetry

Artifacta does not send telemetry data. No usage statistics, crash reports, or performance metrics are transmitted from the application.

No Cloud Processing

All analysis is performed locally on your machine. No file content or metadata is sent to cloud services. No third-party servers process your data.

Local-First Architecture

Artifacta is designed around a local-first architecture:

  • All analysis runs on your device
  • YARA rules are stored locally
  • Cases and reports are saved to your local filesystem
  • No account creation required
  • No internet connection required for core functionality

What Data Stays on Device

Everything. Specifically:

  • Files you analyze
  • Analysis results and findings
  • Case data and investigation notes
  • Generated reports
  • Application settings
  • YARA rules you import

None of this data ever leaves your machine unless you explicitly export or share it.

Open Source

Artifacta is open-source. You can verify these claims by reviewing the source code.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published in the repository and on this page.
