Why Artifacta?

Artifacta performs static analysis of Windows PE files entirely on your machine. No binaries leave your device. No cloud service required. Evidence-backed findings help you understand an executable's observable structure and static capabilities without executing it.

Local-First

Every analysis runs on your machine. Files never leave your device. No uploads, no cloud processing.

Static PE Analysis

Examines Portable Executable structure, imports, sections, and metadata without running the binary.

Evidence-Backed Findings

Every conclusion is backed by concrete evidence. Inspect the data that supports each finding.

No Upload Required

Keep sensitive files on your own machine. No external service ever sees your artifacts.

YARA-X Integration

Scan with YARA rules using the YARA-X engine for pattern matching and classification.

Authenticode Verification

Inspect digital signatures, certificate chains, and signing status of executables.

Investigation Graph

Visualize relationships between imports, exports, resources, and metadata in an interactive graph.

Chronology

Timeline of PE metadata changes, compilation timestamps, and embedded date information.

Case Workflow

Organize executable investigations into cases, preserve analysis history, and compare cases.

Reports

Generate structured reports from your investigations. Export findings for documentation.

How It Works

1. Drop a File

Drag and drop a Windows executable onto Artifacta, or browse to select one.

2. Static Analysis

Artifacta inspects the PE structure, imports, resources, Authenticode signature, and more.

3. Review Findings

Examine evidence-backed findings, explore the investigation graph, and review the chronology.

Important Disclaimers

Signed does not mean benign. Unsigned does not mean malicious. A digital signature proves identity, not trustworthiness. Artifacta shows you the signing status so you can make informed decisions.

Artifacta is not an antivirus. It performs static analysis to help you understand executables. It does not detect, block, or remove malware.
